The macro stress score is at +0.00 for a fourth consecutive week, firmly in the Calm zone, and the macro buffer remains at full strength (+0.50) — the economy pillar is contributing maximum tailwind to the headline posture and nothing in this week's data has moved to change that. Growth is stable but softening at the margin: the real economy score edged down to +0.03 from +0.05 as [GDPNow slipped to 3.6%](https://investinglive.com/news/atlanta-fed-gdpnow-q3-tracker-3-6-vs-3-7-prior/), pulled lower by a further widening in the net exports drag to -2.69 percentage points, though the consumer is doing the opposite — Bank of America card spending per household rose 6.2% year over year in September with broad participation across income cohorts, and [initial claims held at 197,000](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-10-08/us-weekly-jobless-claims-fall-as-layoffs-remain-low) for a fourth straight week near 57-year lows. The "low-hire, low-fire" pattern that has held all year is why the real economy score stays positive despite soft payrolls — weak hiring hasn't tipped into layoffs, and consumer spending is filling the gap. On inflation, the open question from last week is still open: the [September CPI report lands on Tuesday](https://admiralmarkets.com/analytics/traders-blog/us-cpi-preview-september-2026-forecasts-and-the-core-cpi-outlook), but the setup going in is constructive — headline inflation is expected to jump to roughly 3.6–3.7% on a gasoline surge, which is energy noise, while [core is forecast at +0.2% on the month](https://investinglive.com/stocks/us-cpi-preview-energy-set-to-lift-inflation-to-3-7-core-seen-steady-at-2-4/), which would keep the inflation direction unchanged at -0.10 and core CPI at 2.40%. The view changes if Tuesday delivers a core monthly reading of 0.3% or higher — that would start pushing the 6-month inflation direction toward neutral — and if the real economy score keeps drifting slowly toward zero at the same time, both axes would be moving toward stress simultaneously, which is the only path that begins to erode the macro buffer from its current ceiling.
