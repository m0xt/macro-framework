The entire improvement in market momentum this week — from -0.80 to -0.68 — came from bond volatility backing off: the MOVE index fell from 107.29 to 98.47 as markets moved to price an October Fed pause as near-certain, with only a [16–18% probability of a hike at the October 27–28 meeting](https://admiralmarkets.com/analytics/traders-blog/fed-rate-decision-october-2026) priced in by mid-week. That single shift drove financial conditions up +0.52 from -1.32 to -0.79, recovering more than half of last week's record collapse, while the 10-year Treasury yield ticked down marginally to 5.22% from 5.24%. Beneath the surface, growth impulses and breadth both moved in the wrong direction — growth impulses fell to -0.44 and breadth slipped to -0.81 — meaning the headline improvement in market momentum rests entirely on a bond-market repricing, not any broadening of the equity rally or recovery in the real-economy indicators. The critical caveat on that bond repricing is that markets have priced a skip, not a stop: [the Fed and several sell-side forecasters have been explicit that additional hikes remain appropriate before year-end](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/federal-reserve-more-hikes-ahead-after-october-pause-uob-202610081303), which puts a ceiling on how far the MOVE can fall and makes the financial conditions improvement fragile by design. The [Strait of Hormuz remains closed with Iran holding firm on seven conditions and talks at an impasse](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/4/iran-says-strait-of-hormuz-to-remain-closed-until-us-meets-conditions), keeping the stagflationary supply drag embedded in growth impulses and giving the Fed no easier path — weak demand and supply-driven inflation is still the operating environment. The VIX-MOVE divergence flagged last week has narrowed, but from the MOVE falling rather than the VIX rising: equity markets at a VIX of 14.84 are still priced for calm against a 5.22% ten-year yield and breadth at its weakest read of the current cycle, and if December hike expectations get fully repriced into bonds, the MOVE retraces and financial conditions follow it back down. The dashboard's market momentum read moves durably higher only when the rate cycle has a credible endpoint or Hormuz reopens; neither condition is met this week.
