Financial conditions came in at +0.23 and barely moved on the week — VIX at 18.70, MOVE at 70.88, and high-yield spreads at 2.77 are all in comfortable territory — which means credit markets are still treating the Houthi shock as a regional supply disruption rather than a systemic event. The three components remain in a standoff — growth impulses deeply negative, breadth modestly positive, financial conditions comfortably positive — and that tug-of-war is exactly why market momentum is parked in the middle of the CAUTION band rather than tipping toward either LONG or CASH. [Microsoft and Meta report Tuesday](https://www.tipranks.com/news/big-tech-earnings-showdown-meta-and-microsoft-in-focus-ahead-of-july-29-earnings-report), with Apple and Amazon later in the week, and after Alphabet and Tesla were punished for capex-heavy guidance, these results are the most direct lever: revenue beats that show AI spending translating into real growth would firm breadth and nudge market momentum back toward LONG; another round of spending-driven disappointments would pressure breadth and risk pushing the score toward the lower edge of CAUTION.
