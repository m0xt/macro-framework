Market momentum slipped from -0.76 to -0.84 this week and stays deep below zero, as the [S&P 500 fell 2.7% to 7,376.86, its worst week since April 2025](https://www.troweprice.com/personal-investing/resources/insights/global-markets-weekly-update.html), after a midweek artificial-intelligence selloff that also hit broader risk assets. Financial conditions did the damage, worsening from -1.17 to -1.72, because equity volatility jumped, with the VIX rising to 25.64 from 19.42, and the high-yield spread widened to 3.48 from 3.26, even though bond volatility eased to 92.77 from 100.20. Breadth also gave back last week's healing, falling from -0.07 to -0.26, after [Thursday's rout hit technology hardest and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index plunged more than 12% in its worst session since March 2020, with selling pressure spreading across numerous sectors](https://www.troweprice.com/personal-investing/resources/insights/global-markets-weekly-update.html), so the prior hope that losses would stay inside chips no longer holds. Growth impulses were the one bright spot, improving from -1.05 to -0.54, helped by a sharp drop in oil after [Democrats gained control of both chambers of Congress and markets priced a lower chance of near-term Iran escalation](https://www.troweprice.com/personal-investing/resources/insights/global-markets-weekly-update.html), with [Brent tumbling about 17% on the week toward the high-$90s](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/oil-falls-as-trump-says-new-iran-attacks-unlikely.html), while the gap between 10-year and 2-year yields held near 0.38. Last week's tests resolved in opposite directions for markets: the midterms and softer oil premium helped growth impulses, but [Apple beat on fiscal fourth-quarter sales and then cut its holiday margin outlook on higher memory costs](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/apple-results-beat-wall-street-expectationson-strong-iphone-sales-4992898), and the AI trade then broke hard enough that selling spread beyond technology. Momentum turns higher again only if volatility cools and credit stops widening, so the near-term tests are whether this week's AI rebound holds and how stocks digest [December Fed hike odds still near 78% after Friday's strong jobs report](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/traders-raise-bets-fed-rate-134402668.html); another day of selling that pulls banks and high-yield with chips would push financial conditions deeper into the hole.
