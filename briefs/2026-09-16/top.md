The prior brief called today's Fed decision the "fulcrum" — it has now fallen on the wrong side. The [25-basis-point hike to 3.75%–4.00%](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/live/fed-meeting-updates-and-commentary-september-2026) combined with Chair Warsh's open-ended guidance did exactly what the prior brief warned: market breadth crumbled to -1.51, its worst reading of this downturn, market momentum deepened to -0.75, and the headline allocation posture moved from -0.86 to -1.00 — maximum CASH, 0% exposure. The two pillars have now split as wide as they can go: the market pillar is deep in CASH territory with financial conditions barely positive at +0.02 and growth impulses at -0.75, while the economy pillar holds its full macro buffer at +0.50 with stress steady at +0.06 because the hard data — [jobless claims at 206,000](https://www.usnews.com/news/business/articles/2026-09-10/us-unemployment-claims-dip-to-206-000-as-layoffs-continue-to-remain-rare) and [GDPNow tracking Q3 at +1.17%](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow) — hasn't broken to match what markets are pricing in. That gap is the central tension: markets are pricing further pain, but the stress score only rises when the inflation and growth data themselves deteriorate, and this week they didn't. The prior brief named "a hold with credible forward guidance" as the minimum condition to arrest the breadth decay — that condition was not met, and the path back toward CAUTION now runs entirely through breadth recovering on its own, which requires either a natural exhaustion of selling or a clear signal that the Fed is done, and Warsh provided neither. The next hard test is the [mid-October Consumer Price Index release](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm): a third consecutive 0.3% monthly gain would begin bending the six-month inflation trend upward for the first time this cycle, starting to erode the economy pillar's last support — if that lands while breadth is still broken, the divergence compresses in the wrong direction and the posture has no path but to stay at -1.00.
