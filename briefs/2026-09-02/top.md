The headline allocation posture fell from -0.29 to -0.60, crossing the -0.50 line into CASH at 0% exposure — the first full risk-off signal of this cycle, and not for the reason the previous brief anticipated. The payrolls report the prior brief called the "immediate hinge" hasn't printed yet; what moved the dashboard was the [Strait of Hormuz closure](https://straits.live/), with [two supertankers struck September 1](https://www.iransitrep.com/) and daily vessel transits collapsing to near zero — a supply shock that drove breadth to -1.01 and growth impulses to -0.45, pushing market momentum to -0.35. Notably, September rate-hike odds actually [fell back from 66% to roughly 49%](https://polymarket.com/event/fed-decision-in-september-762) on the news, because a supply-driven oil shock is stagflationary — it keeps inflation elevated while damaging growth, making a near-term move harder for the Fed to justify — yet the posture still dropped to CASH because the supply shock more than offset that rate relief. The economy pillar is holding the line for now: the macro buffer sits at its maximum of +0.50, stress is nearly as low as the framework allows at +0.04, and the [August ISM Manufacturing print at 54.6%](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-54-6-august-2026-ism-manufacturing-pmi-report-302865127.html) combined with [GDPNow at 4.8%](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow) show a real economy that hasn't yet absorbed the disruption — but supply shocks typically land in the data on a lag. The CASH signal stays narrow rather than alarming because credit hasn't broken: [high-yield spreads at 2.63%](https://convextrade.com/metrics/bamlh0a0hym2) and a VIX of 16.34 keep stress anchored at +0.04, which means any credible Hormuz de-escalation or agreement restoring shipping lanes is the most direct path back above -0.50; Friday's payrolls matter in second order — a strong number that reignites hike odds extends pressure on breadth without resolving the supply shock, while a miss that deepens the growth-shock read will test whether the macro buffer alone can hold the line on credit.
