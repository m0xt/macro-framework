Market momentum crossed from CAUTION into LONG territory overnight, jumping +0.40 in a single session to reach +0.49 — the two conditions flagged in yesterday's brief resolved on the same day and drove the move together. Growth impulses crossed into positive ground at +0.06, a +0.28 day, as [Treasury Secretary Bessent told CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/08/04/oil-rises-after-selloff-as-talks-to-end-us-iran-war-remain-uncertain.html) there was "a chance we may have a deal today or tomorrow to open the Strait of Hormuz," sending Brent below $79 and WTI toward $75 and extending oil's decline well past the initial $80 break, while [copper climbed to $6.63 per pound](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/copper), its highest in two months. Breadth was the sharper move: it surged +0.87 to +1.05 as the participation signal that had been visible only in equal-weight outperformance finally showed up in actual advance/decline data, with [the NYSE advance/decline line hitting a concurrent new all-time high](https://tradersunion.com/news/market-voices/show/2831421-sp500-breadth-bullish-signal/) alongside [the S&P 500's record close at 7,737](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/04/investing/us-stock-market). The catalyst was [Palantir's 16%-plus pre-market surge on blowout Q2 earnings](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262073539-us-stock-pre-market-pltr-16-mu-5-amd-spacex-earnings-tradingkey), but industrials including Caterpillar rallied in tandem and [most sectors finished in the green](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-aug-4-2026) — that cross-sector lift is what the breadth score responds to, not a single-stock pop. Financial conditions moved just +0.06 to +0.37, with credit spreads at 2.78 and MOVE at 70.88 both holding steady — the one wrinkle is the VIX, which edged up to 16.50 even as stocks set records, a sign that at least some investors are hedging against the deal falling through rather than simply riding the rally. The view is now LONG, but the Hormuz deal is the key fragility: [a June agreement already collapsed within days](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/6/17/oil-prices-continue-slide-amid-hopes-for-peace-opening-of-strait-of-hormuz/), and oil prices are already pricing a deal that has not yet been signed.
