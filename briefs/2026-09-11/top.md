The economy delivered its side of the bargain; markets gave back everything anyway. Compounding the damage, US strikes on [IRGC-linked oil tankers on September 8](https://www.unitedagainstnucleariran.com/analysis/iran-shipping-update-september-8-2026) cut short any Hormuz recovery, and growth impulses dropped to -0.47, leaving market momentum at -0.66. The economy is still growing near 4.4% annualized with inflation not accelerating — macro fundamentals have not deteriorated — but the market pillar is pricing a world where the Fed hikes in four days into a labor market averaging roughly 70,000 jobs per month over the past three months, and that divergence is what is holding the posture at full CASH. What changes it is the [FOMC September 15–16 decision](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm): a hold with neutral or softer forward guidance is the minimum that gives breadth room to rebuild and pulls the posture back toward CAUTION; a 25-basis-point hike with hawkish language would almost certainly keep it below -0.50 through the end of the month.
