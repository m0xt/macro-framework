Market momentum has recovered from -0.85 to -0.32 over the past week — but two of the three drivers carried that move, and the one that hasn't is the one that matters most for whether it holds. Financial conditions led the seven-day gain, rising to +0.37, as the VIX dropped from 17.71 to 14.87 in the days immediately after the [Fed's unanimous 25-basis-point hike](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/fed-rate-decision-september-2026.html) — the hike itself was priced in; what lifted conditions was the relief that uncertainty resolved, with high-yield spreads tightening to 2.68 and [rate volatility staying contained at 81.20](https://streetstats.finance/markets/volatility). Growth impulses recovered sharply to -0.13 (+0.55 over seven days, +0.45 today), as [copper rebounded toward $6.67 per pound](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/copper) after its mid-September slide and [container freight rates held more than 200% above year-ago levels](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/containerized-freight-index) — though the [Strait of Hormuz remains near-closed at roughly 8 transits per day against an 85-per-day baseline](https://straits.live/), so elevated shipping still reflects supply disruption rather than demand strength. Breadth is where the recovery falls short: at -1.19, it moved only +0.01 over the full seven days, with today's +0.33 session gain accounting for almost all of it, and [only about 30% of S&P 500 stocks are back above their 50-day trend lines](https://www.thetrading.tools/market-breadth) even after the [Nasdaq closed at a record 27,122.09 Thursday on a surge in AI names](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/20/stock-market-today-live-updates.html) — AMD hitting $1 trillion in market cap, Intel up 12%, Meta up nearly 12% — that lifted the index while most stocks sat still underneath. The previous brief's watch-point was whether a clear Fed signal that hikes were finished would unlock breadth; that signal did not arrive, with [16 of 18 officials still penciling in at least one more hike](https://www.chase.com/personal/investments/learning-and-insights/article/federal-reserve-raises-rates-officials-signal-one-more-hike-in-2026) and the 10-year yield holding at 5.01%, yet the market chose to treat the hike as a clearing event and pushed higher anyway. At -0.32 and still negative, the view changes if today's breadth improvement sustains over several sessions — more sectors crossing back above trend lines would give the market pillar real footing; if the AI rally fades before it broadens, breadth stays pinned deep in negative territory and takes market momentum back down with it.
