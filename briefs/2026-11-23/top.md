The headline allocation posture dropped from -0.55 to -2.21 this week and stays in CASH at 0% exposure, and the distance back to the -0.50 CAUTION line widened to 1.71 from 0.05, as market momentum fell to -1.95 from -0.29 while the macro buffer held at +0.50 and the stress score only ticked up to +0.09 from +0.06. The pillars moved further apart, not closer: the economy stayed Calm, and last week's open path toward CAUTION had to come from a lasting calm in volatility and credit, but that path closed from the market side after [the S&P 500's worst week since April 2025, a jump in the VIX to 35.89, and a failed artificial-intelligence test](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). [Nvidia's better-than-expected earnings did not hold and the stock fell 23% for the week, and Anthropic's listing, which jumped 17% on Thursday, did not revive the trade](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/sp-500-correction-market-sell-off.html), so last week's hope that the scare was a one-week purge is now off the table. The economy could not offset that shock, and it was not asked to: [October retail sales were merely steady](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/us-retail-sales-increase-moderately-in-october-5033051), [the Atlanta Fed's growth nowcast eased to 3.21%](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow), and [the yearly core inflation rate the dashboard uses is still unpublished](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/cpi-inflation-report-october-2026.html), so the buffer stayed at full strength. Last week's brief said a failed artificial-intelligence test or a jump in yields that lifts equity volatility and high-yield spreads would send the posture back toward a deeper CASH reading; the first part happened, and spreads did widen, even as [the 10-year yield slipped to 5.04 from 5.10 on a flight to safety](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/sp-500-correction-market-sell-off.html). Watch [whether volatility and credit calm through this short Thanksgiving week, and Wednesday's personal spending and inflation reports](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/stock-market-next-week-outlook-for-nov-23-27-2026.html): a hold in high-yield spreads and a retreat in the VIX would start to lift the posture off these deep CASH lows, while another volatility spike or a hot inflation reading that begins to erode the buffer would keep exposure at zero.
