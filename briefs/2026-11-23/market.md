Market momentum collapsed from -0.29 to -1.95 this week, as [the S&P 500 fell 8.61% to 6,910.39, its worst week since April 2025, and closed in correction territory, more than 10% below its record](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). Financial conditions did most of the damage, sliding from -0.93 to -2.84, because equity volatility surged, with the VIX jumping to 35.89 from 19.30, the high-yield spread widened to 3.52 from 3.20, and bond volatility rose to 106.31 from 98.79. Breadth flipped from a support to a deep drag, dropping from +0.19 to -1.59, after [selling spread beyond chips into small caps, banks, and transports, and Nvidia ended the week with a 23% loss even after better-than-expected earnings, including a 14% drop on Friday when Reuters reported that the Securities and Exchange Commission is examining the company's accounting](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/sp-500-correction-market-sell-off.html). Growth impulses also deteriorated sharply, from -0.14 to -1.43, as every input turned negative, led by the jump in volatility, copper falling to 5.48 from 6.02, and weaker risk appetite, even as the gap between 10-year and 2-year yields steepened slightly to 0.28 from 0.24 and [the 10-year yield slipped to 5.04 from 5.10 as investors sought the safety of Treasuries](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/sp-500-correction-market-sell-off.html). Last week's tests resolved against markets on the two points that matter for momentum: volatility did not stay calm through [Nvidia's earnings and the Anthropic listing](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/stock-market-next-week-outlook-for-nov-23-27-2026.html), and credit started widening again. [Anthropic jumped 17% on its Nasdaq debut Thursday and closed at $97.17, but that did not revive the artificial-intelligence trade, and the stock gave back a large share of the gain on Friday](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/anthropic-ipo-ant-live-updates.html). The read improves if equity volatility cools and high-yield spreads stop widening through this short Thanksgiving week; another spike in the VIX or a further turn higher in credit spreads would keep financial conditions deep in the hole.
