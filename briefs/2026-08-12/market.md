Market momentum climbed back to +0.62 — up +0.07 on the day and +0.13 over the past week — driven almost entirely by a sharp rebound in growth impulses, which recovered +0.14 on the day and +0.37 over seven sessions on the back of two concrete developments. Copper [reached a new all-time high of $6.77 per pound on August 7](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/06/copper-jumps-to-its-highest-level-ever-what-the-metal-is-telling-us-.html), settling back near $6.65, as Congo's new export ban on copper concentrate tightened global supply while electrification and data center demand kept a floor under prices; separately, the [Baltic Dry Index climbed to 3,089](https://www.bairdmaritime.com/shipping/dry-cargo/bulkers/baltic-dry-bulk-index-charges-to-two-month-peak-as-vessel-rates-jump) — its highest since early June, up 13% on the week — evidence that bulk shipping is finding routes around Hormuz rather than seizing up. The Iran watch-point from yesterday's brief has not resolved: [talks between Tehran and Oman remain close but unfinished](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/iran-deliberates-hormuz-arrangement-amid-uncertain-prospects-with-us), with Iran still conditioning reopening on the US lifting its naval blockade, which means growth impulses are recovering despite Hormuz being closed, not because of it, and the full reopening upside remains on the table. [July CPI landed this morning](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/july-cpi-broad-heat-in-the-core-202508121356) with a benign 0.2% headline — holding year-over-year at 2.7% — but core came in hotter than expected at 0.32% monthly and 3.1% annually, the highest year-over-year core reading since February, driven by a rebound in airfares and medical costs; markets absorbed it without flinching, though if that core trend persists it raises the probability that the 10-year yield stays pinned near 4.72% and eventually pressures breadth. Breadth itself remains the strongest component at +0.82, and the seven-day decline of -0.23 is consolidation after the S&P 500 hit a record above 7,737 in early August, with [more than 71% of its members still above their 200-day moving averages](https://thrasheranalytics.substack.com/p/breadth-852026) — the rally is broadly supported, not narrowing. Financial conditions are essentially unchanged at +0.61, with the VIX at 15.28, bond market volatility at 70.88, and [high-yield spreads at 2.70%](https://convextrade.com/metrics/bamlh0a0hym2) — credit is not warning. The view stays LONG; what would shift it is a sustained hot-core inflation trend that pushes rates meaningfully higher while breadth thins out, or a breakdown in Iran talks that reverses shipping's recovery and caps growth impulses just as they rebuild.
