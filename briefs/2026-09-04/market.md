The August payrolls print — [22,000 jobs](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/non-farm-payrolls/news/483228), less than half the 53,000 consensus — resolved the previous brief's central question exactly as the miss scenario predicted: the Hormuz growth shock has moved from the shipping trackers into the labor data, and the rate hike debate has broken toward patience. [Governor Waller said Thursday he would support holding rates at the September meeting](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/fed-governor-waller-indicates-he-will-support-holding-rates-steady-at-september-meeting.html) if disinflation continues, which sent rate hike odds below 55% and drove financial conditions to +0.64 today (+0.21 on the day) as the VIX fell to 14.32 and bond volatility eased to 74.68. Market momentum lifted to -0.08 (+0.15), but the seven-day read of -0.33 shows what the week actually cost: breadth, down -0.59 over seven days, sits at -0.51 even after today's +0.18 bounce, meaning the [defensive sector rotation](https://www.fxempire.com/forecasts/article/sp500-sector-rotation-holds-as-warsh-reprices-september-fed-forecast-1620062) into health care and energy that broke breadth below zero has not reversed. Growth impulses remain at -0.37 (-0.28 on the week) and have no near-term catalyst: [the Strait is on Day 187](https://straits.live/) with transit volume at roughly 7% of its pre-crisis baseline, [copper has fallen to its lowest level since July](https://www.riotimesonline.com/copper-markets-latam-wednesday-september-2-2026/) under a US dollar index above 118, and the 10-year Treasury at 4.79 keeps term premium elevated even as the 2-year yield lags at 4.39. High-yield credit spreads at 2.66 reflect a market that is calm rather than optimistic — the corporate bond market is not pricing in stress, just not signaling recovery either — so financial conditions at +0.64 are doing real work but are held up by a VIX that is now directly conditional on hike odds staying low. The [August inflation report on September 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/03/rates-waller-jobs-inflation-policy) is the next binary: Waller named it explicitly as the condition for his hold, so a clean disinflation read is what gives breadth room to rebuild and could push market momentum above zero for the first time in over a week, while a sticky reading reopens the September hike debate and tests whether today's financial conditions bounce survives contact with the data.
