The [August jobs report](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/jobs-report-august-2026.html) — 162,000 new positions against a 53,000 consensus — moved growth impulses from -0.23 to -0.04 in a single session (1d +0.19), and it is the main reason market momentum climbed to +0.08. The payrolls beat touched nearly every input growth impulses tracks: the 10-year/2-year yield spread is a positive 0.43 percentage points, not inverted; credit quality firmed; and sector rotation tilted modestly toward cyclicals and industrials, which is the same direction breadth needs to keep moving. Breadth is still negative at -0.33, down slightly on the day, but its seven-day read improved +0.22 as industrials, staples, and energy absorbed more of the market's weight — the previous brief's worry about leadership narrowing to megacap technology is easing at the margin rather than deepening. Financial conditions at +0.62 still carry the pillar nearly alone, though both the daily and seven-day reads are drifting lower, and the VIX moving to 15.30 from 14.53 signals that markets are beginning to price in data risk ahead of the Fed's meeting. The [Hormuz conflict escalated again over the weekend](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/9/7/iran-war-live-tehran-to-announce-new-hormuz-shipping-route-in-coming-days) — US strikes on Iranian tankers, Iranian missiles at Navy ships — capping how far growth impulses can recover, since shipping disruption feeds directly into that index. [PPI arrives Wednesday and CPI Thursday](https://fortune.com/2026/09/06/fed-rate-hikes-inflation-data-ppi-cpi-august-tariffs-oil-prices/) with [September hike odds near 60%](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxfeddecision/fed-meeting/kxfeddecision-26sep): a hot reading would likely crack the credit-and-volatility calm that financial conditions depend on and drag market momentum back toward zero; a soft reading extends the runway and gives breadth a genuine chance to push closer to neutral — the same binary the previous brief flagged, now just 48 hours away.
