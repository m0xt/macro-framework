Market momentum jumped from -0.83 to -0.29 this week, as the [S&P 500 rose 2.5% to 7,561.67, its best week since August](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/stock-market-next-week-outlook-for-nov-16-20-2026.html), even after [Nvidia fell about 13% when its chief executive resigned](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/stock-market-next-week-outlook-for-nov-16-20-2026.html). Financial conditions did most of the healing, improving from -1.72 to -0.93, because equity volatility cooled, with the VIX falling to 19.30 from 25.64, and the high-yield spread tightened to 3.20 from 3.48. Bond volatility went the other way, rising to 98.79 from 92.77, as [the 10-year yield climbed to 5.10 from 4.84 and the 2-year jumped to 4.86 from 4.46 after Tuesday's hot inflation report, leaving a December Fed hike fully priced in](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/treasury-yields-fed-rates.html). Breadth flipped from a drag to a support, rising from -0.26 to +0.19, after [investors rotated into other technology names such as Apple, Advanced Micro Devices, and Broadcom instead of abandoning the artificial-intelligence trade](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/stock-market-next-week-outlook-for-nov-16-20-2026.html), so last week's worry that selling would keep spreading beyond chips did not play out. Growth impulses also improved, from -0.51 to -0.13, helped by tighter credit and [a second weekly drop of about 2.5% in oil, with Brent settling at $95.84 and U.S. crude at $88.56](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/oil-prices-set-for-second-weekly-decline-as-trump-to-stay-with-iran-blockade.html), though copper stayed the main drag and the gap between 10-year and 2-year yields flattened to 0.24 from 0.38. Last week's tests resolved in favor of markets on the two points that matter for momentum: volatility did cool and credit stopped widening, and the rest of the market held up even as Nvidia fell. The improvement holds if stocks stay this calm through [Tuesday's Nvidia earnings and the Anthropic listing later in the week](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/stock-market-next-week-outlook-for-nov-16-20-2026.html); a fresh spike in bond volatility or a turn higher in high-yield spreads would put financial conditions back in the hole.
