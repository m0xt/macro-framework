The weak July jobs number that had held September rate hike odds near 42% was overturned Friday: the [August employment report](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/us-jobs-report-august-2026) showed 162,000 new positions against a forecast of roughly 53,000, the strongest monthly gain since March, and [pushed market-implied hike odds near 50-50](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxfeddecision/fed-meeting/kxfeddecision-26sep) after briefly surging above 59% on the release — a complete reversal of the hold-friendly setup the previous brief was working from. The [August ISM Services reading of 55.4](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/services-pmi-at-55-4-august-2026-ism-services-pmi-report-302868046.html), the 26th consecutive month in expansion, compounded the hawkish signal: the service economy is growing briskly, the Fed has little cover to stand pat, and together the two data prints explain why market momentum's seven-day trend is -0.14 even as the daily print ticked a fractional +0.01 to +0.06. That +0.06 is being held up by financial conditions alone (+0.69, seven-day -0.11): credit spreads have held tight at 2.65 and the VIX has stayed at 14.53, so the calm in credit and volatility markets has not cracked despite a higher rate path — but growth impulses (-0.22, seven-day -0.17) and breadth (-0.29, seven-day -0.13, flat on the day at +0.00) are both tracking lower over the week, because strong payrolls data does not translate into broader market participation when it implies more rate pressure ahead. Cyclicals remain absent from leadership, the [Strait of Hormuz is on Day 189](https://www.iransitrep.com/) with Iranian ballistic-missile strikes following September 1 US airstrikes adding escalation rather than resolution, and the dollar above 118 keeps copper under pressure — structural headwinds in growth impulses that a strong payrolls print does not touch. September 11 CPI is now an even sharper gate than the previous brief described: a hot reading would confirm the hike, compress the credit and volatility calm that financial conditions depend on, and almost certainly pull market momentum below zero; a soft print resets the hold case and gives breadth its first genuine path back toward zero.
