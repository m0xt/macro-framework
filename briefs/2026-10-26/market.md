Market momentum improved from -1.26 to -1.07 this week, but it is still deep in negative territory: markets are pricing less stress than seven days ago, not an all-clear. Almost all of the gain came from breadth, which jumped from -1.02 to -0.54 as money left the AI leaders for the rest of the market, with an [S&P 500 index that excludes AI up about 6.6% in October while the full index is down roughly 0.4%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/stock-market-today-live-update.html) and [small caps up 2.00% on the week while the S&P 500 lost 0.47%](https://www.troweprice.com/personal-investing/resources/insights/global-markets-weekly-update.html). That is the broadening the previous brief said was missing, but it is broadening by subtraction: the [semiconductor index lost 6.7% on Thursday alone after Meta raised the floor of its spending plan to $135 billion, burned cash for the first time in more than a decade, and said it would sell a new class of shares to pay for AI](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/steep-bill-for-ai-buildout-punishes-meta-other-tech-favorites-4967187). Growth impulses recovered from -1.39 to -1.14, a bounce from very weak levels rather than a turn, because riskier stocks are still losing badly to defensive ones and cyclicals are still trailing safe sectors. Financial conditions were the one driver that got worse, falling from -1.38 to -1.54: the VIX barely moved (19.45 versus 19.99 a week ago), but bond volatility rose to 103.31 from 101.10 as the 10-year yield [posted its biggest one-day drop since March 2023 on Thursday and then bounced back toward 5% on Friday](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/stock-market-today-live-update.html), while [credit default swaps on CoreWeave widened to levels generally considered distressed](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/stock-market-today-live-update.html) and the high-yield spread sits at 3.40. On last week's three watch-points, AI credit stress has not been contained so much as moved up the ladder, with [Meta's own default insurance hitting a record](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/steep-bill-for-ai-buildout-punishes-meta-other-tech-favorites-4967187), and Hormuz got worse, with [Brent settling at $116.72, its highest close since April 29 and up 6% on the week](https://petra.gov.jo/en/news/oil-prices-jump-more-than-3-to-six-month-high), while the Fed test is still ahead. Market momentum keeps climbing only if the rotation keeps broadening while bond volatility calms, so the next read hinges on Wednesday's Fed decision, where markets price [about a 10% chance of a hike now and a 73% chance by December](https://rateprobability.com/fed), and on [Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon earnings](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/us-equity-futures-climb-as-crude-oil-price-dips%3a-markets-wrap/92273033) showing whether the AI spending bill looks payable; an oil spike that pushes the 10-year back above 5% would undo the week's progress.
