Stress holds at +0.49 — Calm on the 0–10 scale, -0.04 on the week — and the macro buffer stays at +0.48, essentially unchanged but with materially stronger confirmation underneath. That resolves the prior brief's core tension: the labor market signal at 187,000 initial claims and the business activity surveys are now telling the same story, and the real economy score has moved to -0.65 (7d +0.06), its best reading in several weeks. With the PMI beat in hand, the Q2 advance GDP estimate on July 30 is more likely to confirm the picture than disrupt it — but a print materially below 1.5% annualized alongside a July CPI upside surprise on August 12 is still the combination that turns the inflation direction axis positive and starts eroding the macro buffer.
