The macro stress score is at +0.00 for a fifth consecutive week, firmly Calm, and the macro buffer remains at full strength (+0.50) — but the scenario last week flagged as the key risk has now arrived. The [September CPI report](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/cpi-inflation-report-september-2026.html) delivered a second consecutive +0.3% monthly core reading, pushing the year-over-year rate to 2.60% and shifting the inflation direction from -0.10 to +0.00: the six-month cooling trend is over. The stress score hasn't moved because the inflation direction landing at neutral is not the same as inflation accelerating — the framework needs that axis to go positive before it registers as stress — but the disinflation tailwind that had been holding the inflation direction below zero is now spent. On growth, the real economy score edged lower again to +0.02 from +0.03: [September retail sales jumped 0.8%](https://www.usnews.com/news/business/articles/2026-10-15/retail-sales-up-a-better-than-expected-0-8-in-september-as-shoppers-remain-resilient) on the headline but collapsed to only +0.2% once gasoline is stripped out, with a 6.4% surge at gas stations doing almost all the work, while [initial claims ticked up to 199,000](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/daily-labor-report/us-weekly-jobless-claims-rise-1k-to-199k-est-200k) — still below 200,000 for a fourth straight week but no longer falling. The shelter component of core CPI [rose 0.4% in September](https://piedmontcrescentcapital.com/september-cpi-2026-gasoline-moves-the-headline-services-move-the-trend/), contributing roughly half the monthly core gain, and this is the [mechanism that bears watching](https://www.morningstar.com/economy/septembers-hot-cpi-report-shows-price-pressures-broadening): shelter disinflation had been the single biggest force pulling the six-month inflation direction lower, and if it has stalled, the path back to a negative reading closes. What changes the view is now clear — a third consecutive 0.3%+ monthly core print flips the inflation direction positive, and if the real economy score simultaneously continues its slow slide toward zero, both axes of the stress score would be pointing up at once, which is the only path through which the macro buffer begins to erode.
