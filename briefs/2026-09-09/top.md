The headline allocation posture has fallen to -0.42 — just 0.08 points above the CASH threshold — and for the first time this week the two pillars are moving in opposite directions: the market pillar has deteriorated sharply while the economy pillar hasn't budged. Breadth collapsed from -0.33 to -0.84 in Monday's session, pulling market momentum to -0.17, with the [Hormuz exclusion zone](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/9/7/iran-war-live-tehran-to-announce-new-hormuz-shipping-route-in-coming-days) and September's seasonal headwinds compounding a market already repricing rate risk — financial conditions fell to +0.43 and the VIX climbed to 15.72, leaving credit spreads as the last buffer keeping that component above zero. The previous brief's central open question — whether ISM services prices paid at 72.6 would flow through to consumers — still has no data answer, but the breadth rout looks like markets voting yes in advance: the selloff has the shape of pre-positioning rather than reaction, and with the [Minneapolis Fed now estimating tariff pass-through is adding 0.2–0.4 percentage points to core prices](https://www.minneapolisfed.org/article/2026/initially-delayed-the-pass-through-of-tariffs-to-consumer-prices-has-arrived), that fear has a factual foundation. The economy pillar is the only thing holding the posture in CAUTION: macro buffer at +0.50 and stress at +0.04 reflect a labor market that surprised to the upside and [GDPNow still tracking third-quarter growth at 4.7%](https://www.atlantafed.org/research-and-data/data/gdpnow), but those readings reflect lagged data, and the inflation direction axis at +0.00 leaves no room for deterioration before the buffer starts eroding. Tomorrow's [Producer Price Index](https://www.bls.gov/ppi/) sets the tone — July's reading came in unchanged month-over-month, and a hot August print would sharpen the fear heading into [Thursday's Consumer Price Index](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm) — where a core monthly reading at or above 0.3% would validate the breadth selloff, push the VIX higher, begin tilting the inflation axis positive, and likely deliver enough damage across both pillars to break through -0.50 to CASH; a soft sequence on both gives breadth room to recover and market momentum to return toward zero, stabilizing the posture — though with [Fed hike odds at 56%](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/fomc-september-2026-odds-rate-201618784.html) and Hormuz risk still unresolved, any path back toward LONG depends on data the market has not yet seen.
