The headline allocation posture improved from -0.91 to -0.74 — the first move higher in several sessions — but the call is unchanged: full CASH, and the improvement is narrower than it looks. The entire gain came from the market pillar absorbing Thursday's CPI shock: the VIX retreated from 17.84 to 15.84, financial conditions recovered from +0.00 to +0.20, and market momentum crossed back above its own -0.50 floor to -0.49; breadth and growth impulses edged higher to -1.24 and -0.42 respectively, but neither reflects a genuine trend reversal — seven sessions of outflows have left the breadth reading deep in negative territory regardless of one day's partial relief. On the economy side, the August core Consumer Price Index's [0.3% month-over-month print](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm) delivered a paradox the prior brief anticipated: the annual rate still eased from 2.50% to 2.40% as an older reading rolled out of the window, keeping the macro buffer fully intact at +0.50 and stress at +0.07, but that same above-consensus monthly pace is what hardened [hike odds to 60–70%](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/fomc-september-2026-odds-rate-163505675.html) — the identical force suppressing the market pillar from recovering further. The GDPNow collapse from 4.4% to 1.16% adds a second wrinkle: the economy brief correctly identifies it as [largely a trade-deficit accounting effect from surging AI equipment imports](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-03/us-trade-gap-widens-to-largest-since-early-last-year-on-imports) rather than a demand breakdown, and the real economy score reflects that with only a modest move to -0.09, but it means the Fed would be tightening into a sub-2% headline growth print — the clearest near-term catalyst for stress to climb from its current benign reading. Both pillars converge on the same verdict: the [September 16 FOMC decision](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm) is the binary that resolves the posture, with a hold giving financial conditions and breadth room to keep rebuilding toward CAUTION while a 25-basis-point hike with hawkish language almost certainly drives market momentum back through -0.50 and returns the headline closer to last session's -0.91 low.
