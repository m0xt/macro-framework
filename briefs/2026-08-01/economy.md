The real economy score has nudged back below zero to -0.08 after touching +0.12 on July 31, with the most likely driver being the [Q2 GDP advance estimate from the BEA, released July 30 at 1.5%](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-advance-estimate-2nd-quarter-2026) — below the 1.8% consensus and a step down from Q1's 2.1% — which weighed just enough on the composite's spending and activity inputs to pull it back below center; the GDPNow z-score of +1.38, reflecting the initial Q3 estimate of 5.0%, continues to anchor the growth read and prevent any meaningful stress rebuild.
