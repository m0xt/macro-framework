Market momentum's brief recovery failed, sliding from -0.32 to -0.62 as financial conditions reversed course and erased the post-hike bounce — that component swung from +0.37 to -0.28 (7d -0.63) in five days. The driver was the bond market: rate volatility, measured by the MOVE index, surged from 81.20 to 96.00 after the [10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.18%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/treasury-yields-oil-inflation-fed.html) — a near-two-decade high — as [stronger-than-expected PMI data pushed markets to price the funds rate above 4.8% by late 2027](https://streetstats.finance/rates/fedfunds), while high-yield credit spreads widened to 2.80, adding further drag on financial conditions. Growth impulses fell in tandem, back to -0.58 from -0.13: [copper is sitting around $6.66 per pound](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/copper), the dollar index at 119.51 is weighing on commodity demand signals globally, and the yield curve's steepening came from long rates rising rather than short rates falling — which reflects inflation worry, not growth confidence. The previous brief's watch-point partly resolved in the right direction: breadth improved +0.58 over seven days to -0.99, the only driver moving positively, as [more stocks recovered above their trend lines even as the S&P 500 nears a record on gains concentrated in AI names](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/23/stocks-breadth-signals-sp) — new 52-week lows still outnumber new highs across the index, so the improvement is real but thin. The sharpest tension in the dashboard right now is the divergence between equity and bond volatility: the VIX sits unchanged at 14.87 while the [MOVE index just posted its biggest weekly surge in over a year](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/treasury-volatility-set-for-biggest-jump-in-year-as-bonds-churn), meaning bond traders are pricing meaningful turbulence that equity markets haven't acknowledged. The view turns more constructive if breadth widens further into cyclical sectors and VIX stays contained — that stabilizes financial conditions and gives market momentum a foundation to build on; if equity volatility reprices to match what bond markets are already saying, financial conditions fall further and market momentum has room to worsen.
