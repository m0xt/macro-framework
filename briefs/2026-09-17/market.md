Market momentum fell another -0.11 to -0.85 — and the most revealing detail is that breadth dropped -0.25 further to -1.76, its worst reading this cycle, on the [same day the S&P 500 posted a 0.59% price gain](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/stock-market), meaning a small group of large-cap names lifted the index while more stocks slipped further below their 50-day trend lines underneath. The Fed [delivered the expected 25-basis-point hike to 3.75%–4.00% Wednesday](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/16/business/live-news/federal-reserve-interest-rate-september), but Chair Warsh's press conference [left further hikes explicitly on the table](https://www.thestreet.com/fed/fed-raises-rates-signals-another-hike-2026) — 16 of 18 officials penciled in at least one more — and the [10-year Treasury yield settled at 5.00%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/treasury-yield-bond-market-fed-decision.html), its first sustained hold at that level since 2023, which keeps the discount rate on equities elevated and makes it harder for weaker sectors to attract buyers. Financial conditions slipped a further -0.04 to -0.02, with [high-yield spreads at 2.76](https://govspending.org/series/BAMLH0A0HYM2/) and the VIX at 17.71 extending a seven-day drift of -0.39 — no spike, but a steady pressure that has now pushed this component into negative territory. Growth impulses fell -0.03 to -0.78: copper briefly [hit an all-time high on the London Metal Exchange](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/commodities/articles/copper-surges-time-high-tariff-152401505.html) earlier this month before retreating sharply on tariff uncertainty, leaving that input volatile and inconclusive, while the [Strait of Hormuz remains near-closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis) with [Iran-Oman talks stalled over fee-collection rights](https://www.timesofisrael.com/iran-oman-hormuz-deal-wont-immediately-reopen-strait-source-tells-iranian-media/) and no near-term reopening in sight. Market momentum has now dropped -0.51 over seven days with all three drivers still moving lower: until breadth actually turns — something that requires either a natural exhaustion of selling or a Fed signal that hikes are finished — the score has nowhere obvious to go but further negative.
