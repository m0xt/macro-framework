[Fed Chair Kevin Warsh used his Jackson Hole speech on August 28](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/kevin-warsh-jackson-hole-fed-inflation-rate-hike.html) to sharpen his inflation warning and frame September as a live rate-hike decision, [driving market odds for a September hike from below 40% to roughly 66%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/jackson-hole-fed-chair-kevin-warsh-hawkish-rate-hikes-analysts.html) and ending the broad rally that had pushed breadth to +0.96 just three weeks ago. Breadth is now at -0.55, a -0.78 decline on the week, as [technology and semiconductor names led the repricing](https://articles.stockcharts.com/article/mindfulinvestor-2026-08-weakening-market-breadth-could-signal-trouble-ahead/) and the share of S&P 500 members holding above key moving averages fell sharply — this single component drove market momentum negative at -0.04 (-0.20 on the week). Growth impulses are also in the red at -0.25 (-0.04 on the week, -0.18 on the day): the Baltic Dry from the last brief's watch-point partially recovered, [bouncing toward 2,926 by late August](https://www.bairdmaritime.com/shipping/dry-cargo/bulkers/baltic-dry-bulk-index-back-to-near-two-week-high-as-rates-rebound), and [copper held near record levels at roughly $6.62 per pound on tightening London warehouse stocks](https://www.mining.com/copper-price-holds-near-record-as-london-warehouse-bidding-war-looms/), but defensive sector rotation and rising yield-curve pressure are now outweighing those commodity tailwinds. Financial conditions are the one component still in the green at +0.67, and they are actually stronger on the week (+0.21), with [high-yield spreads tightening to 2.60%](https://convextrade.com/metrics/bamlh0a0hym2), the VIX at 14.92, and bond volatility at 75.32 — credit is not confirming a breakdown, which is what keeps this a repricing rather than a genuine stress event. The Hormuz watch-point is no closer to resolution: [Iran's newly declared Strait Authority continues to demand US concessions before reopening](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/8/28/iran-war-live-tehran-prepares-conditions-to-open-strait-of-hormuz), with Qatar's mediation still active but no deal in sight. The view turns on the next two data points: [Friday's jobs report](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-29/us-jobs-report-seen-backing-warsh-view-of-labor-market) — economists expect roughly 55,000 payroll additions after July's surprise dip — and the September 16 Fed decision; a strong number validates Warsh's hike and puts further pressure on breadth, while a soft reading would ease rate-hike odds and give financial conditions room to pull market momentum back toward zero.
